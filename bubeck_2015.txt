Title: Convex Optimization - Algorithms and Complexity (Bubeck, 2015)
Source: arXiv:1405.4980

Summary: This monograph by Sebastien Bubeck presents the main complexity theorems in convex optimization and their corresponding algorithms. It starts from black-box optimization theory and progresses to structural and stochastic optimization. The work is strongly influenced by Nesterov's foundational research and covers cutting plane methods and accelerated gradient descent schemes.

Key topics: gradient descent, mirror descent, Frank-Wolfe algorithm, dual averaging, FISTA for optimizing a sum of smooth and non-smooth terms, saddle-point mirror prox, and interior point methods.

Relevance to machine learning: These algorithms form the mathematical backbone of how modern ML models are trained, especially for convex loss functions where global optima can be guaranteed.
